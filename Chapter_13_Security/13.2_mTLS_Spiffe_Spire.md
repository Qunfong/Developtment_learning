## 1. The Physics of mTLS


In standard TLS, the client verifies the server. In **mTLS**, the server *also* verifies the client.
. **The Perimeter:** Security moves from the firewall (L3/4) to the **Identity Layer (L7)**.
. **The Tax:** The initial handshake is expensive, but subsequent packets use **AES-NI** hardware acceleration.


---


## 2. Source Archaeology: SPIFFE and SPIRE


> [!abstract] The Mechanics: SVID
. **SVID:** A short-lived X.509 certificate.
. **SPIRE Agent:** Lives on every node. It verifies the pod's identity (using K8s UID) and issues an SVID automatically.
. **Rotation:** SVIDs are rotated every hour. If a pod is compromised, the cert is useless within minutes.
