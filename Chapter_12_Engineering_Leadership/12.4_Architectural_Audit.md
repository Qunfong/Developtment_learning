## 1. The Requirement: The Whiteboard Duel


**The Problem:** Hubris. Engineers build complex systems because they "Sound good," but fail when a single physical constraint (like a cross-NUMA stall) is pointed out.


### 1.1 The Defense Law
. **The Rule:** You must justify every component in terms of **Physical Limitations**.
. **Success Example:** "I chose Kafka because its Zero-Copy physics is the only way to saturate our 10Gbps NIC with 5% CPU usage."
